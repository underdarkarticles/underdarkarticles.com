## Session Notes

Session six starts with all of the party together and pushing forward. We are have been lost venturing through the tunnels for days. The entire time the group has proceed forward, they have felt as if they have been followed by something omniscience. The party is starving and thirsty; they see large blue, purple, and green mushrooms all over the room. The floors are covered in algae and slippery to get from they are. The party sits on their butts and slides down the path; once at the bottom, the floor is red from some sort of crimson.


Allan touches a large yellow mushroom and his hands draw up, his starts to swell up. Around this time, the party finds an even larger purple mushroom than before and stands taller than a dwarf. Belmore sees something move behind these new larger mushrooms. Glorp takes 2HP of damage to his calf from a red worm. Belmore rips the worm off of Glorp. In the middle of this, Belmore fired a fireball towards the direction he saw something moving. This caught the grass on fire and got the worms to flee; it also revealed a path.
